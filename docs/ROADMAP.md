# 9-Level + Boss Roadmap

Short-session design: each level 1–3 minutes.

| Level | Theme              | New Element              | Goal                     |
|-------|--------------------|--------------------------|--------------------------|
| 1     | Green Hills        | Basic stomp + collect    | Teach core loop          |
| 2     | Underground        | Moving platforms         | Timing                   |
| 3     | Sky Islands        | Flying enemies           | Vertical movement        |
| 4     | Forest             | Denser enemies           | Pattern recognition      |
| 5     | Cave               | Spike hazards            | Precision                |
| 6     | Castle Approach    | Turrets / projectiles    | Dodging                  |
| 7     | Factory            | Moving platforms + mix   | Combined skills          |
| 8     | Final Stretch      | All enemy types          | Endurance                |
| 9     | Boss Arena         | Shadow Grok              | Multi-phase fight        |

## Boss: Shadow Grok (Level 9)
- Large version of a Grok Bot shape.
- Phases:
  1. Walks and jumps (stompable)
  2. Fires projectiles
  3. Shape-shifts or summons mini blobs
- Defeat condition: 3–5 stomps or power-up hits.
- Short and readable on GBC resolution.

## Power-ups (reusable)
- Jump Boost (temporary higher jump)
- Speed
- Invincibility (short)
- Projectile (optional later)

Reuse the same actor templates and scripts across levels to stay within hardware limits.
