# Levels 2-10 and the MUSE boss

> **PyBoy-only, untested on hardware.** Everything here was built with
> GB Studio 4.2.2 CLI and checked in the PyBoy emulator only.

## Levels
Levels are generated by `scripts/build_level1.py` from section recipes
(F two floor blobs, A raised ledge, B pit, C float staircase, D flyer lane,
E bolt tower). Each level gets longer and harder; pits appear in the
staircase from level 7. Aim: 1-3 minutes each.

## Boss technique
MUSE is an original fuzzy cream creature (drawn in code; no reference art
traced). It is 10x10 BG tiles (80x80 px) drawn into a band of the arena
background with replace-tile events. It enters from the right and hops
between three spots (x = 2, 5, 8 tiles, 24 px apart), resting between hops
(180 / 140 / 110 frames by HP) with a telegraph first (cheeks blink twice
plus a low beep). An invisible hitbox actor follows it for contact, stomp
and shot checks; two small cheek sprites add colour. HP (8) is shown as
heart pips in the HUD. Each landing fires a staggered burst of 3 one-tile
sparks; every 2nd hop releases a blob at the boss edge facing Grok Bot.
Defeated blobs drop ammo, and the player can shoot the boss.
Defeat: shake, flash, "MUSE DEFEATED!" and the win scene.

### Why hops, and the speed numbers (PyBoy, game frames per 900 ticks)
Each REPLACE_TILE_XY costs roughly 1/7 of a frame (a VRAM-safe tile copy),
and a game frame is lost whenever the boss thread is still busy. Chunking
only spreads the cost: a hop costs ~18 game frames however it is paced
(5 writes per frame, then Wait 1). So MUSE moves less often instead.

| Build | Boss fight speed |
|-------|------------------|
| 80bfa34 (8 px steps, 10 writes/frame) | 64-67% (47% at low HP) |
| 16 px steps every ~72 frames, 5 writes/frame | 75-76% |
| 2 writes/frame, same steps | 84% |
| **current: 3 spots, 24 px hops, 5 writes/frame** | **91% (HP 8), 88-89% (HP 4 or 2)** |
| Level 9 reference | 100% |

Options tried earlier: one full redraw per step (~58%), per-column redraw
with yields (~65%). A sprite-built boss was rejected (80 px wide would
overflow 10 sprites/line).

## Stomp rules
The hitbox's On Hit Player script ignores contact while $5 (hurt lock) or
$16 (player-safe) is set. A landing needs plat_vel_y > 0 and the player's
Y no lower than 8 px below the hitbox Y.
- MUSE not immune: HP-1, then MUSE is stomp-immune ($29) for 26 rest
  ticks of 4 frames (~104 frames plus any hop redraw), with blinking cheeks.
- MUSE immune: no damage, no hurt; Grok Bot is just bounced off.
- Either way Grok Bot bounces high and is slid off toward the nearer side,
  clear of the hitbox (GB Studio has no horizontal-impulse event, so x is
  eased over 6 airborne frames), and is player-safe ($16 = 1, $5 = 2) for
  20 frames.
- Electron hits use their own 20-frame boss grace ($30); they never make
  Grok Bot safe from sparks or blobs.
HP resets to 8 on a fresh visit; a respawn reload (3 hits) deliberately
keeps the damage already done.

## Budgets / GBC notes
- Levels 2-9: 11 BG tiles each, scanline peak 10/10.
- Level 10: 176 BG tiles (tight); win scene 43.
- Boss sparks are one 8x16 hardware sprite each (6 px orb), launched 5
  frames apart on rows 8 px apart, below the HUD: mid-screen worst case 8.

## Simplifications / known issues
- MUSE moves in 24 px hops between three spots, and tears for ~30 frames
  while it redraws each hop.
- The boss fight still runs at ~88-91% speed (PyBoy measurement).
- Stomp-only win takes ~32 s in PyBoy (1 HP per immunity window). Shots
  are faster: 4+ electrons at a 20-frame grace can take several HP in a
  couple of seconds, so a perfect mixed run is roughly 8-10 s.
- Released blobs walk after spawning and can wander into the boss sprite
  (blobs do not collide with the boss).
- The immunity blink is cheeks-only (blinking the BG-tile body would cost
  ~100 tile writes per blink); it pauses during a hop redraw.
- One defeat flash; score saturates. Moving platforms not used.
- Tests used PyBoy with memory writes to place Grok Bot above MUSE (then a
  real fall); not tested on hardware.

### Music
- Cause found: every level scene re-issued Play Music, and gbvm's
  `vm_music_play` always re-inits the driver, so the song restarted at
  Level 1, at High Ridge, and at every new level. Fix: the title starts the
  track and sets var 32 (MUSIC_VAR); level scenes only start it if var 32
  is 0. Verified by hooking `_vm_music_play`: v3 = 4 starts on title → L1 →
  High Ridge → L2 → respawn; v4 = 1 (title only).
- Sound effects are all legacy beeps with mute mask 8 (channel 4, noise),
  priority minimal; they never cut the melody channels 1-2.
- The music ISR runs at a constant 4.28 calls per frame on every build and
  level, so it is not starved by CPU load (PyBoy).

### Chip rule
Firing needs the chip collected in the current level and not lost since:
- New game, every new level (2-9) and a respawn after a death: chip 0,
  ammo 0, unpowered.
- A hit while powered clears power, ammo and chip (so chip == glow).
- Moving from Green Hills to High Ridge (same level) keeps it.
- The debug warp no longer grants the chip.
- Boss arena exception: chip is set on entry; ammo from blobs is the only
  rule (and at least 4 electrons on entry).
v3 bug: the chip lasted "for the run" (across levels, after a hit and after
a death), and the debug warp set it, so Grok Bot could fire with no glow.

### Sprite MUSE (fluid movement)
Options considered:
- (a) sprite-built MUSE — chosen. 48x64 metasprite actor, 22 hardware
  sprites (empty corner tiles dropped), at most 6 per row. The pink cheeks
  are two tiles inside the body sprite that use the Muse Pink palette
  (cream/pink/dark), so there are no separate cheek actors; blinking
  swaps to frame 1 (no cheeks). Moved 1 px/frame (2 px/frame at HP ≤ 2)
  with Set Position every frame, between three spots 48 px apart. Measured:
  B_X advances 1 px on almost every frame (two 1-frame hitches per 48 px walk).
- (b) smooth BG scroll of a locked arena: rejected. MUSE would have to be
  the only thing on the BG layer, so the floor/ledges and HUD would need to
  be sprites or a window/LYC split; GB Studio 4.2.2 has no event for per-
  scanline scroll, so it needs engine code.
- (c) keep BG tiles with a 1-tile step: still ~1/7 frame per tile write.
Behaviours kept: contact damage, stomp with immunity and knock-off, safe
bounce, 3-spark staggered bursts, blob releases next to the body, ammo
drops, HP hearts, defeat flash and win screen.
OAM budget: HUD 8 + MUSE 22 + player 2 + sparks 3 = 35; with 2 blobs or
drops on screen as well it can reach the 40-sprite limit, and the last
actors drawn may be dropped for a frame (not seen in PyBoy tests).

## v5 — smaller, smooth MUSE (PyBoy-only, untested on hardware)

- MUSE is redrawn as original 24x32 art (cream fuzzy body, dot eyes, smile, pink cheeks; faces its walk direction). It is 6 hardware sprites on one palette. The 48x64 art, cheek actor, hitbox sprite and boss tileset art are gone (the tileset is now just sky plus heart pips).
- **Stutter root cause (v4):** MUSE was moved by a script thread that called Set Position and then waited a frame. GBVM threads don't resume in step with rendering, so on busy frames a step repeated or got skipped (the PyBoy log showed 25,25 and 62→64). The 22-tile body also pushed OAM to 40 and 12 sprites on a line, so tiles dropped and flickered on hardware.
- **v5 motion:** a single actor and metasprite, moved by the engine's Move To at a fixed speed: 1 px/frame, or 2 px/frame when HP is 2 or less. All tiles update in actors_update on the same frame. It patrols the whole arena from x 20 to 124. At each end it does a spark burst and, every second pass, a blob release, then rests briefly, gives a cheek-blink telegraph and turns. Stomp immunity counts down on VM timer 2 (16-frame ticks, 7 ticks ≈ 112 frames) because the boss thread is blocked during a move. A defeat cancels the move.
- Re-tuned for the new size: hitbox bounds (-2,-12,20,27), stomp zone, spark launch points (x -4/+12, y -24/-32/-40), blob release tiles and knock-off targets.

## v6 — hit, ammo and score audit (PyBoy-only, untested on hardware)

- **Score stopped at 9,990.** add_score clamped it to SCORE_MAX = 9990 because the HUD had only 4 digits, and raw points would overflow the 16-bit signed variable at 32,767 anyway. Now the score variable counts tens of points, the HUD has 5 digits (a new digit actor at tile 6), and the cap is 99,990. Score carries across levels and respawns, and resets only on a new run. Text boxes print `$score$0`. There is no high score and no save RAM.
- **Hurt grace was invisible.** An unpowered hit waited 60 frames with no blink (only powered hits blinked). It now blinks for the same 60 frames.
- **Hits from empty space.** Every enemy and the player used a full 16x16 box, so contact registered up to ~6 px before the art touched. The boxes now fit the art: blob (2,2,12,14), flyer (1,3,14,10), tower (0,4,16,12), player 12 px wide (2,0,12,16). Contact now starts ~2 px before the art touches, down from ~6 px.
- The extra HUD actor dropped one ledge blob in Level 1 High Ridge (20-actor limit), and the generator's fit step trimmed 4 electrons in the densest levels (30 → 26).
- Ammo was audited with no bugs found: it clamps at 0, caps at 8, the HUD redraws on every change, it resets on a new level and on a hit or death, it is 4 at boss entry, and it is spent only when a shot launches (the 5-projectile pool can't fill: ≤1 shot plus ≤3 sparks or bolts).
