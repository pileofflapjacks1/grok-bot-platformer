## Play Level 1
The game is called **GROK BOT HERO** (the project name in `GrokBotPlatformer.gbsproj`; the file name is unchanged so existing clones keep working). Open `GrokBotPlatformer.gbsproj` in GB Studio 4.2 or newer and press Play.

Boot flow: two boot cards (dark backdrop, centred text, a hopping Grok bot, about 2.3 s each with fades; "Created by @CardCaptain", then "With the help of Grok 4.7 and Grok Bot Carty G"; a three-beep jingle on the first; Start or A skips straight to the title; Game Over returns to the title without them) → Title ("GROK BOT HERO", PRESS START) → Picker → Level 1. Start or A leaves the title right away. On the picker (on-screen hints: LEFT/RIGHT: SHAPE, UP/DOWN: COLOR), Left/Right cycles circle, square, heart, star, triangle, teardrop, oval, and blob (wrapping both ways), Up/Down cycles white, red, yellow, teal, and A starts. The default is circle + white, so Start, A gets you playing. The choice is kept through pits and Game Over. Game Over returns to the title with lives, ammo, score, power-up and the hurt lock fully reset. After "LEVEL 1 CLEAR" a button press returns to the title.

Level 1 is two scenes and 340 tiles wide, about five times the previous run. Green Hills leads straight into High Ridge. A full clear is a few minutes, not a 15 second clip.

- Grok Bot uses the locked Grok Imagine style: a solid shape with a thin black outline and two short thick black oval eyes (no mouth, eye whites, pupils, or antenna). Left facing is mirrored.
- Move with the arrows or WASD. Jump with Alt, Z, or J. Hold Control, K, or X to run. That same button fires an electron once Grok Bot is armed.
- Eight Grok Blobs (four per half, two of them before each silicon chip), with eyes, a belly, brows, and feet. Stomping one hides it and bounces you. A shot also removes the blob, without the bounce. A side touch knocks you back, shakes the screen, and counts as one hit. Three hits cost one life. You go back to the start of the current half (Green Hills or High Ridge, like a pit), and the hit count resets. There is about a second between hits, so standing on a blob does not count as three.
- Four Zap Bats (two per half): small winged bots that bob back and forth over a 4-tile loop. Contact works like a blob: stomp it, shoot it, or take a hit.
- Three Bolt Towers in High Ridge (x=60, 110, 143), spaced so no screen holds two, and bolts live 0.7 s (about 11 tiles), so even when running a bolt from one tower is gone before the next can fire. Once a tower is inside the camera frame (Grok Bot within 9 tiles) it fires a small energy bolt at Grok Bot every 2 seconds (GB Studio Launch Projectile, aimed at the player). A bolt or a touch hurts like a blob's side touch. Stomping a tower hurts like a touch: only electron shots destroy a tower (+500).
- Score (variable 8) in the top middle, four digits: blob 100, Zap Bat 200, Bolt Tower 500, electron 10, chip 1000, level-clear bonus 1000. The final score is shown with LEVEL 1 CLEAR.
- Eight electrons. Touching one adds it to the ammo badge in the top-right. That badge is a blue square with a two-digit count, including 00 before you collect any. You do not need all of them to finish.
- Two silicon chips per half: one on the first low ledge after the opening blobs (Green Hills x=54, High Ridge x=48) and a spare later (x=118 / x=140). Collecting one powers Grok Bot up (`powered` variable 9): the outline glows teal (yellow on a teal body) by swapping sprite palette slot 0. Every hit counts toward 3; the third hit costs a life and reloads the half at its start, powered or not. An earlier hit while powered costs the power-up and all electron ammo (the ammo badge drops to 00) instead: the glow goes, Grok Bot blinks for about a second and cannot be hurt during it. Grab another chip to power up again. The electrons are after the first chip. Collect the chip before the shots will fire. Until then, the run button only runs. Each shot spends one electron. The score tops out at 9990 (the HUD's last digit is a fixed 0). The jump stays at 3.5.
- You start with 3 lives. The top-left badge is a black square with a red heart and that number. Each half has one pit. Falling in takes away control (blank platformer state, and B is unbound until respawn), Grok Bot visibly drops off the bottom of the screen over about half a second, then the screen fades out, it costs a life (and any power-up), and fades back in at the start of that half. Losing a life (pit or hits) reloads that half at its start, like classic platformers: enemies, electrons and the chips come back, while lives, score and ammo are kept. You respawn unpowered: a pit costs the power-up, and a third hit costs it with the life. Ammo is capped at 8 (the number of electrons), so electrons that come back after a reload cannot push the badge past 08. You get about 1 s of invulnerability after the reload, shown by Grok Bot blinking, and the music keeps playing. At 0 lives the screen fades back in, says "Game Over", and returns to the title screen; lives, ammo, the chip, the hit count, score, power-up and the hurt lock are reset for the next game.
- The template song starts again at the beginning of each half.
- Level complete: a white roadster (a low two-seat electric hypercar silhouette: wedge profile, dark glass canopy, black wheels with gray hubs; original procedural art, no badge, logo or wordmark; `assets/sprites/roadster.png`, Roadster White palette in sprite slot 4) is parked near the end of High Ridge (x=158). Touching it locks movement and unbinds fire, Grok Bot hops in (the player is hidden), the car rolls forward, hops and does a kickflip (one full spin through 6 procedurally rotated frames), then climbs to just under the HUD, then blinks out (GB Studio clamps relative moves at y=0, so it cannot physically leave through the top). The +1000 clear bonus is added, one text box shows "LEVEL 1 CLEAR" with the final score, and a button press goes straight to the title (there is no separate clear scene any more). A 2-tile exit trigger at the very end is kept as a fallback if you jump clean over the car.

Jump feel is set in `project/engine_field_values.gbsres`: jump velocity 3.5, a slightly lower hold-gravity, coyote time, and jump buffering. See `docs/PLATFORMER_PLUS.md`.

The sky has clouds and distant hills. Those bands scroll slower than the ground. One screen shows the player, the life badge, the ammo badge, and at most one blob plus a couple of electrons.

`python3 scripts/build_level1.py` regenerates the level art, scenes, and those settings. It does not remove the GB Studio template fonts or UI.

Fonts, the dialogue frame, emotes, and the placeholder `actor*.png` sprites come from the GB Studio blank project template (Chris Maltby, MIT).

### Budgets (per Level 1 scene)
- Actors: Green Hills 18 / 20, High Ridge 20 / 20 (GB Studio's `MAX_ACTORS`; the 5 pinned HUD actors (lives, ammo, 3 score digits) count). Triggers: 2 / 30. GB Studio only runs actors near the screen, and the generator fails the build if any screen-wide window could put more than 10 hardware sprites on one row, counting the player, one electron shot in flight (fire is capped at one shot per 0.6 s, each living 0.55 s) and, near a Bolt Tower, its bolt (two bolts if two towers are close enough for one bolt to outlive the run between them at 19.2 tiles/s; none are now). The current peak is 10/10. **Bigger-sprites prototype (branch sprites/bigger-prototype):** the player is 24x24 (3 sprites per line, 2 rows of 8x16 tiles, collision box still 16 px wide, 22 px tall); enemies, chips and electrons fill more of their 16 px cell; the electron shot, tower bolt and ammo badge (one digit, ammo is capped at 8) are 8 px wide (1 sprite per line), which pays for the wider player. Full 24x24 enemies would need 17 sprites on one line in Green Hills.
- Sprite palettes: 6 / 8 slots (Grok Bot, Blob Red, Electron Blue, Chip Gray, Roadster White, Flyer Violet; the old Coin Gold slot was freed when coins were removed and reused for the roadster). The power-up glow and the picker colors swap slot 0 instead of using new slots.
- Sprite VRAM, in 8x16 tiles: Green Hills 76 / 192, High Ridge 112 / 192 (bigger-sprites prototype: 93 / 192 and 129 / 192, player slot 24) (the roadster kickflip frames are 32). GB Studio reserves one player slot sized to the largest player sheet (8), not one per shape, and loads each other sheet once. Color-only mode splits sprite tiles across both VRAM banks.
